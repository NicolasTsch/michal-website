Biography

As the winner of First Prize at the 6th International Evgeny Svetlanov Conducting Competition 2026, awarded by unanimous decision of the jury, Michal Oren also received the Orchestra Prize, presented by the musicians of the City of Birmingham Symphony Orchestra. She was named a Classic FM Rising Star 2026 and is currently Calleva Assistant Conductor of the Bournemouth Symphony Orchestra.

In the wake of her competition win, she has already been offered engagements to appear with orchestras such as the Royal Liverpool Philharmonic Orchestra, Orchestre National des Pays de la Loire and the City of Birmingham Symphony Orchestra.

During the 2026/27 season, Oren conducts a series of concerts with the Bournemouth Symphony Orchestra, as well as concerts with the Israel Camerata Jerusalem and the Israel Sinfonietta Beer Sheva.

Oren has worked with distinguished conductors including Jaap van Zweden, Johannes Schlaefli and Alexander Shelley at the Gstaad Conducting Academy. She participated in the Taki Alsop Conducting Fellowship Mentoring Programme and was Female Conductor Trainee at Opera North. She was also selected for the Royal Philharmonic Society's Women Conductors' Programme with the Royal Northern Sinfonia and the Young Conductors Showcase with the BBC Scottish Symphony Orchestra.

Her achievements include First Prize at the 4th International Academy and Competition of Orchestra Conducting in Estoril, Portugal, as well as Second Prizes at the 5th Koussevitzky Conducting Competition in Grosseto, Italy and the 3rd International Orchestral Conducting Competition "Universidad de Almería", Spain. She has received scholarships from the Robert Anderson Trust and the America-Israel Cultural Foundation.

Beyond the concert stage, Oren has a particular interest in the dialogue between music and the visual arts. She founded the Museum Orchestra in collaboration with the Petach Tikva Museum of Art, creating performances that explore musical interpretations of visual art exhibitions. This initiative reflects her commitment to artistic storytelling and interdisciplinary collaboration.

Born in Tel Aviv, Oren studied orchestral conducting and clarinet performance at the Buchmann-Mehta School of Music at Tel Aviv University. She subsequently completed a Master's degree in Orchestral Conducting at the Royal College of Music in London as a Victor and Lilian Hochhauser Scholar, followed by a Professional Diploma in Conducting at the Royal Academy of Music, where she studied with Sian Edwards and received the Bianca Falcone Sorrell Award.
